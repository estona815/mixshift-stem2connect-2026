Limits, sources and AI assistance
Use matched, consistently defined groups. Each total must be positive; missing groups are not imputed. Use 2–12 groups and at most 1,000,000 observations per period. A success must be a subset of its total. These arithmetic checks do not verify your data, a causal effect, statistical significance or a confidence interval. Audit exports contain every input label and count: review them before sharing.
This is a new educational implementation of established rate-decomposition ideas. See US Census: Standardization and Decomposition of Rates and Kitagawa (1955). No algorithm invention or measured learning improvement is claimed. All examples are synthetic.
